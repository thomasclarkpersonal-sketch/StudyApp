# A10 · Requirements Catalog

Owner: Systems Analyst. Stage 2, written 2026-10-21. Catalog entry: A10. Approved and baselined at CP2 (2026-10-21; CP2 corrective addendum approved 2026-10-23; `DEC-015`, `DEC-016`; updated via `CR-002`, `CR-003`).

Each of the 89 requirements (`REQ-F-001`–`REQ-F-066` and `REQ-N-001`–`REQ-N-023`) is a testable statement using the preferred terms from [`glossary.md`](glossary.md) (`A18`), stored in [`requirements.csv`](requirements.csv) beside this file (protocol §5.3).

- **Priorities (MoSCoW):** `Must` (`R1` MVP), `Should` (`R2`), `Could` (`R3`), matching `A01 §5.1` and `DEC-010`.
- **Cohesive story-level requirements & documented departure from `A10.1` strict single-clause granularity (`A04 §5`, `CR-003`):** Rather than splitting tightly coupled clauses of a single user story or pipeline stage into separate `REQ-F` rows (for example `REQ-F-002` read-only Canvas ingestion and its local-folder fallback, `REQ-F-009` Reviewer context isolation plus Pass 1 and Pass 2 rubric evaluation, or `REQ-F-010` the 2-revision-round cap and verdict reconciliation), `A10` groups each end-to-end story capability under one cohesive `REQ-F-nnn` ID (`66` `REQ-F` + `23` `REQ-N` = `89` total) as a documented departure from catalog check `A10.1` (*"No compound requirements (two behaviours joined by 'and' or 'or')"*). To prevent partial verification or coarse change-request impact analysis (`G2.1`, `R5`, `R7`), every sub-clause of a multi-clause requirement is decomposed into atomic Business Rules (`BR-001`–`BR-045` in [`business-rules.md`](business-rules.md)), Decision Table rules (`DT-01`–`DT-06` in [`decision-tables.md`](decision-tables.md)), and distinct Acceptance Scenarios (`AT-001`–`AT-137` in [`acceptance-scenarios.md`](acceptance-scenarios.md); e.g., `AT-003` for Canvas read and `AT-030` for local-folder fallback under `REQ-F-002`). Under the [`traceability-matrix.md`](../00-governance/traceability-matrix.md) verification rule, a requirement may be marked `Verified` at Gate `G4`/`G5` only when **100% of its linked in-release `BR-nnn` rules and `AT-nnn` scenarios** pass; if any sub-clause is deferred or fails, the requirement remains unverified (`Open` / `Partially Verified`), and any `CR-nnn` modifying a multi-clause requirement scopes its impact analysis to the specific `BR-nnn` and `AT-nnn` sub-clauses affected.
- **Proposals for CP2 (`G2.13`, `DEC-016`):** Any requirement not explicitly expressed by the user in the seed, source documents, or `Q-01`–`Q-17` / `CP1` answers—or marked *(proposed)* in the seed (`ASM-037`)—is labelled `**(Proposal for CP2)**`. All 14 `**(Proposal for CP2)**` items were approved by the student at `CP2` (`DEC-016`); the `**(Proposal for CP2)**` tags are retained below as permanent provenance markers (`G2.13`). In `A22` (`Sourcing Decision`), options are judged only against user-stated requirements; `**(Proposal for CP2)**` items are shown separately (`G2.16`).
- **Approval-style items (`G2.16`):** Seed §10 items phrased as "Always ask the user first before…" are recorded in §4 as governance decision points under protocol R6, never as `REQ`s.

## 5. Explicitly out-of-scope items (`A01 §5.2`)

Every seed non-goal (`[SEED §11]`) and user exclusion (`[USER Q-13]`, `[USER Q-14]`, `[USER Q-17]`) is excluded from `REQ-F` and `REQ-N` (matching `SC-X01`–`SC-X09` in `A01 §5.2`, written `X1`–`X9` in the CP1/CP2 messages):
- **`SC-X01` (`X1`):** Multiple users, accounts, deck sharing, or collaboration (`[SEED §11]`).
- **`SC-X02` (`X2`):** Native iOS or Android applications (`[SEED §11]`).
- **`SC-X03` (`X3`):** Scraping university portals other than HKUST Canvas, writing or submitting work to Canvas, or reading Canvas discussions, rosters, or other students' data (`[SEED §11]`, `DEC-009`, `ASM-049`, `ASM-061`). *(Note: read-only Canvas access for the student's own course files, pages, assignments, announcements, and syllabi is in scope as `REQ-F-002` per `DEC-009`.)*
- **`SC-X04` (`X4`):** Two-way synchronisation with Anki; only one-way TSV export (`REQ-F-066`) is in scope (`[SEED §11]`).
- **`SC-X05` (`X5`):** Generating flashcards or quiz questions from general knowledge rather than the student's course material (`[SEED §11]`).
- **`SC-X06` (`X6`):** Having the tutor write or rewrite assessed coursework (`[SEED §11]`, enforced by `REQ-F-051` and `REQ-N-023`).
- **`SC-X07` (`X7`):** Pipeline-free on-the-fly daily quiz question generation (`[SEED §11]`).
- **`SC-X08` (`X8`):** The Mandarin module (`[USER Q-13]`, `DEC-007`).
- **`SC-X09` (`X9`):** Importing existing flashcards or review history from Anki or other tools (`[USER Q-14]`, `ASM-014`).
